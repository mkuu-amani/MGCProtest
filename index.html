Mkuu Amani

Mkuu Amani is a UK-based documentary photographer, videographer, and writer whose work chronicles social justice, community advocacy, and the lived experiences of African and African-Caribbean communities across the West Midlands.

Operating from Walsall, Amani uses his lens to capture the raw energy of grassroots activism, public rallies, and cultural milestones—from regional marches to intimate portraits of local changemakers. Alongside his visual storytelling, he is a respected spoken-word artist and community journalist who served as the Walsall Poet Laureate in 2014.

Through his photography, video production, and written journals, Amani preserves vital local history and amplifies underrepresented voices across Britain.
